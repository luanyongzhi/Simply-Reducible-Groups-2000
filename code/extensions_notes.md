# Complete candidate covers for the (6,4) and (7,3) strata

The counts in this note are candidate counts and orbit counts under specified parent stabilizers. The final group-isomorphism classification is performed separately by the canonical-graph and quadratic-refinement scripts.

## Why the construction is exhaustive

Let G be a stem class-2 SR group of order 1024, with V=G/G' of dimension m and W=G' of dimension r, where (m,r)=(6,4) or (7,3). Choose any central subgroup N<=G' of order 2. Then P=G/N is an SR group of order 512, still of class 2, with dim(P/P')=m and dim(P')=r-1. Its centre may grow: **non-stem parents must be included**.

In the dual scalar-polar model, the (r-1)-dimensional form space of P is a hyperplane N0 in the r-dimensional form space of G. After identifying P with a representative from the supplied complete 512 census and making an invertible change of basis in V, every target form space is N0+<A> for some nonzero coset A+N0 in Alt(V)/N0.

Therefore it suffices to extract the polar spaces from all relevant 512 parents, enumerate every one-dimensional extension of each fixed polar space, test the MF rank identities, the zero common radical condition, and existence of ambivalent refinements, and then classify the resulting covers up to genuine equivalence. The refinements are solved afresh; we do not assume a chosen parent quadratic refinement lifts.

This argument uses the completeness of the supplied order-512 SR catalogue. It does not require the old order-1024 enumeration scripts.

## Input extraction (GAP 4.15.1)

`extract_parents.g`, using `SR2_Extract` from `sr_class2.g`, reconstructs the 95 class-2 SR parents from their supplied SmallGroup IDs and writes `parents512_forms.txt`.

Each row has

    SmallGroup_ID m r B1 ... Br

where each B encodes the upper-triangular entries of a scalar alternating form, in lexicographic order (12,13,...,1m,23,...). Redundant bases are reduced by exact row reduction over F2.

| Target | Parent groups, including non-stem | Distinct coordinate parent spaces | Nonzero quotient vectors per parent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| (6,4) | 51 (37 stem, 14 non-stem) | 9 | 4095 |
| (7,3) | 33 (11 stem, 22 non-stem) | 8 | 524287 |

The other 11 parents are three of type (5,4) and eight of type (8,1); those strata are handled elsewhere.

## Direct enumeration results

`extend_parents.cpp` enumerates all nonzero vectors in Alt(V)/N0, using zero parent-pivot coordinates as the unique coset representative. A one-dimensional F2 subspace has exactly one nonzero vector, so this enumeration has no within-parent duplication. Different parent spaces can produce the same target space; target RREF bases are used for exact coordinate deduplication.

| Target | Quotient vectors inspected | MF hits | MF + stem hits | MF + stem + reality hits | Distinct coordinate SR form spaces |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| (6,4) | 36855 | 599 | 424 | 358 | 356 |
| (7,3) | 4194296 | 435017 | 414782 | 290490 | 290488 |

All 356 spaces for m=6 have quadratic-refinement affine dimension 6; all 290488 spaces for m=7 have dimension 7. Thus each admits respectively 64 or 128 refinements before the polar stabilizer acts. Neither 356 nor 290488 is a group-isomorphism count.

Local measured times were approximately 0.02 seconds and 3.4 seconds respectively; these are machine dependent. Exact output is retained in `extend_m6_results.txt` and `extend_m7_results.txt`.

## Two independently generated parent-stabilizer covers

`parent_subgroup_orbits.cpp` implements the induced linear action of invertible V-transformations on Alt(V)/N0. Every proposed generator is explicitly checked to preserve N0. With rows g_i representing the images of basis vectors, scalar matrices transform by B -> g B g^t. The code constructs this exterior-square action, reduces modulo the parent space, and carries out exact breadth-first searches of the surviving quotient vectors.

Two generator sources were used:

1. A subgroup generated by verified single and paired elementary transformations and coordinate swaps. Fullness of this subgroup is unnecessary for completeness: it merely yields a possibly redundant cover.
2. Full parent-polar stabilizer generators exported by the separately checked canonical-graph code in `parent_polar_auts_m6.txt` and `parent_polar_auts_m7.txt`.

| Target | Generator source | Parentwise orbit representatives | Coordinate-distinct representatives | Sum of orbit sizes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| (6,4) | Elementary subgroup | 11 | 9 | 358 |
| (6,4) | Full parent stabilizer | 11 | 9 | 358 |
| (7,3) | Elementary subgroup | 45 | 44 | 290490 |
| (7,3) | Full parent stabilizer | 25 | 24 | 290490 |

The orbit-size sums agree exactly, parent by parent, with the direct enumeration. This supplies a useful independent coverage check. The 44-space and 24-space m=7 covers can both be globally canonicalized; they must yield the same final polar orbits. Parentwise orbit counts themselves still contain cross-parent isomorphism duplication.

Representative-file format:

    m r affine_dimension B1 ... Br

## Reproduction

Place `sr_class2.g` next to `extract_parents.g`, or retain the project directory layout. Then run GAP 4.15.1 with the SmallGrp package installed:

    gap -q -b -A extract_parents.g

Compile the two C++17 programs:

    g++ -O3 -std=c++17 extend_parents.cpp -o extend_parents
    g++ -O3 -std=c++17 parent_subgroup_orbits.cpp -o parent_subgroup_orbits

Run direct enumeration:

    ./extend_parents 6 < parents512_forms.txt > extend_m6_results.txt
    ./extend_parents 7 < parents512_forms.txt > extend_m7_results.txt

Run the elementary-subgroup reductions:

    ./parent_subgroup_orbits 6 < parents512_forms.txt > subgroup_m6_results.txt
    ./parent_subgroup_orbits 7 < parents512_forms.txt > subgroup_m7_results.txt

Run full-parent reductions using the exported generators:

    ./parent_subgroup_orbits 6 parent_polar_auts_m6.txt < parents512_forms.txt > fullparent_m6_results.txt
    ./parent_subgroup_orbits 7 parent_polar_auts_m7.txt < parents512_forms.txt > fullparent_m7_results.txt

The source uses GCC/Clang's unsigned 128-bit integer type to encode radicals of seven-dimensional spaces. No floating-point computation or randomized selection is used.

## Remaining interpretation step

The candidate-cover argument is complete once the input 512 census is accepted. Exact group counts additionally require global GL(V)-equivalence of the polar spaces and full stabilizer orbits on their quadratic refinements. Those tasks are delegated to the canonical-graph classification code; this note does not infer isomorphism from numerical fingerprints.
